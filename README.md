# Mega Datum HR Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data for Mega Datum to uncover key HR metrics, workforce demographics, employee performance, and attrition patterns. The objective is to generate data-driven insights that help improve employee retention and optimize organizational performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor overall workforce metrics and employee attrition
* Identify key factors contributing to employee turnover
* Analyze employee performance across departments
* Support strategic HR decision-making with actionable insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and KPI tracking
* **Python** – Exploratory data analysis and visualization
* **SQL** – Advanced querying and workforce data extraction

---

## 📊 Power BI Analysis

Developed a comprehensive HR dashboard highlighting workforce trends and performance indicators.

**Key Features:**

* Employee hiring trends over time
* Active vs. inactive employees by department and job role
* Demographic analysis (age groups, gender, ethnicity, marital status)
* Salary insights and workforce diversity metrics
* Job, environment, relationship satisfaction, and work-life balance tracking
* Self vs. manager performance ratings
* Attrition analysis by department, job role, tenure, overtime, business travel, and hire date

**Key Measures Created:**

* Total Employees
* Active / Inactive Employees
* Attrition Rate (%)
* Average Salary
* Performance & Satisfaction Scores
* Last and Next Review Dates

---

## 🐍 Python Analysis

Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA) to better understand employee behavior and organizational dynamics.

**Analysis Included:**

* Distribution of categorical variables such as gender, marital status, department, and business travel
* Age statistics (min, max, average, distribution)
* Gender balance visualization
* Workforce distribution across departments and roles
* Education level analysis
* Satisfaction level comparisons across employee groups
* Relationship between satisfaction and attrition
* Self vs. manager rating distributions
* Identification of high- and low-performing departments
* Gender pay gap and diversity analysis
* Evaluation of how diversity impacts satisfaction, performance, and attrition

---

## 🗄️ SQL Analysis

Used SQL to extract insights and answer complex workforce questions.

**Key Queries:**

* Top job roles by employee count
* Longest-tenured employees
* Department-level attrition rates
* Salary comparisons across education levels
* Employees earning above average salary
* Distance-from-home analysis
* Department salary averages and maximum salaries
* Monthly tenure calculation from hire date
* Gender distribution by education level and field
* Highest-paid employees by role and education

---

## 📈 Key Outcomes

* Built an end-to-end HR analytics solution combining SQL, Python, and Power BI
* Identified major factors influencing employee attrition
* Enabled data-driven recommendations to improve retention and workforce performance
* Delivered executive-ready dashboards for strategic decision-making
